Defend the big blind on the price
The big blind already has a blind in and acts last preflop, so against a button open it defends a very wide range and folds only the true trash.

Your seat BIG BLIND

The button opens and folds to the big blind with KQ offsuit. What price do you get, and how wide should you defend?

Why Closing the action with a blind already in lays a low price, so the big blind defends very wide against a button open. KQ offsuit sits comfortably inside that range. Call and see a flop with a hand that can improve.
