Offsuit — 4 Premium Design Directions

Each direction was generated then refined across 5 adversarial waves (premium-vs-tacky → radical reduction → reimagine the table without felt → motion & fluidity → cohesion + AA contrast). The green-felt cliché is banned in all of them. Each demo shows Home + the live Table with a Motion toggle. Open each, then tell me which direction (or which mix) to take to implementation.

Direction 1
Broadsheet — Monochrome Editorial
A printed strategy broadsheet in the dark: ink-on-paper typography, hairline rules, two inks + one jade signal colour, serif figures. The card is a fine-press index card; the table is a typeset data page, not felt.
Direction 2
Obsidian Jewel
A private-bank app on true-OLED black. Depth by luminance + hairline, one champagne glow for the player to act, ivory cards as the one rich object. Weighted, critically-damped motion. Luxury by restraint.
Direction 3
Gallery Light
A premium LIGHT theme — bone canvas, one oxblood accent, museum whitespace. Cards are the only framed objects, drawn as fine editorial prints; seating is drawn in space, not with a shape. Proves premium ≠ dark.
Direction 4
Kinetic Minimal
A calm near-black instrument where motion is the identity. Villains are a monogram + stack + two card-backs on the void; the table is implied by a shared elliptical baseline, never drawn. Still at rest, alive on interaction.

All four are static mockups (before/after of Home + Table). Nothing here is wired into the app yet.
