4.5 bb into 4.5 bb. What equity do you need to call?

The answer
The method, step by step
  1. The pot was 4.5 bb before the bet, and the bet is 4.5 bb.
  2. Your call lands in the pot too, so what you are buying is 4.5 + 4.5 + 4.5 = 13.5 bb.
  3. Your share of it is your call over that total: 4.5 / 13.5.
  4. 4.5 / 13.5 = 33.33% — that is the equity the call needs.
  5. As a frequency you can picture: win it about 1 time in 3.
