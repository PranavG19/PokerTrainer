The research's one-miss grace is offered as a decision to evaluate, not built. |

## What "premium" means here (the checks every mock passes)
1. It matches `demo/glass` tokens. No new hex values in screen styles; new tokens live in `vision.css` with a role.
2. It fits 900×640 and 1100×760 with no page scroll **and no dead band**: content starts at the top of the coat.
3. There's one bone primary per screen and one hero figure.
4. Every object is drawn, not a placeholder.
5. Every personalized line names its data source (see the source line under each mock and `vision/personalization-sources.md`).
6. Dee appears at most once in the content, plus the 20px title-bar perch (chrome). It's silent in-hand.
