Static PNGs: motion and states are inferred from what the frame shows (affordances, implied state, and any sign of a capture taken mid-transition). They are not observed, so treat them as low-confidence.

## The 5 most important fixes overall

1. **Kill the vertical-centring dead bands and the tab-thumb misalignment in the Family Y toolbar and layout.** Several screens (Home, Verdict, Story, Lesson) open with a 170–330px empty band. The selected-tab thumb sits 15–25px off its label on every Family Y screen. Top-align content 24px under the toolbar, and position the thumb from the label's bounding rect without a first-paint transition.
2. **Fix text and object collisions.** The orbit line cuts through venue labels (Story B). The result tray overlaps the hero cards and D puck at 1100 (pair03-B). Bo's seat straddles the rail (pair03-A). The Dee disc indents the "Serious" header (pair02-B). Reserve tray height, mask strokes behind labels, and inline the mascot.
3. **Enforce "bone = cards plus the ONE primary".** The push-chart cells (Study), the "your call / your 100" segments (Lesson), the "Refresh Blockers" header button (Profile B) and the ghost-rail "You" bar all spend bone. Move these to fill-3 neutrals. Conversely, give the commit tray (solo10) its missing bone Commit.
4. **One identity system.** Two toolbars, two Dee glyphs (bone face vs D wedge), two names (Sam vs Rowan Ellis), two table styles (oval felt vs floating seats), two avatar glyphs inside a single chat, and a wrong selected tab (Home on the Dee page). Pick the bone-face, floating-seat, "You"-tab system. It scored about a point higher.
5. **Keep personalisation about the player, not the system.** The highest-scoring screens quote the player's own rule, leak and rival (Letters, Rivals, Style, the Verdict with "Your rule, from 3 October"). The lowest expose system jargon or spec copy: "Rim colours are role colours", "Bone enamel rim · from 4 assessments", "2 spare wicks", "Chalk", "Ghost Rail". Restore the rule callout on the verdict, cut the internal vocabulary, and make dark-on-dark collectible thumbnails legible (Locker A, Trophy shelf) so earned items read as rewards.
